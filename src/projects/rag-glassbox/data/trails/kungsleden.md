# Kungsleden

## Overview
The Kungsleden, or King's Trail, runs about 440 km through Swedish Lapland between Abisko in the north and Hemavan in the south. Few people walk the whole length in one go. The northern section from Abisko to Nikkaluokta, about 105 km over six or seven days, is by far the most popular because it passes Kebnekaise, Sweden's highest mountain.

## Route
From Abisko the trail follows a river valley through birch forest, then rises onto open fells above the tree line. It passes the huts at Alesjaure and crosses the Tjäktja pass, the highest point of the northern section at about 1,150 m. South of the pass, walkers can leave the main trail at Singi to reach the Kebnekaise mountain station and finish at Nikkaluokta. Further south, the trail continues through Saltoluokta and Kvikkjokk towards Ammarnäs and Hemavan, a much quieter stretch.

## When to go
The hiking season is short, from late June to mid-September. In summer the midnight sun means it never gets dark, which makes long days easy. Mosquitoes are intense in July. Late August and September bring autumn colours, fewer insects and the first chance to see the northern lights.

## Where to stay
The Swedish Tourist Association, STF, runs a chain of mountain huts and stations roughly a day apart, with bunks, kitchens and sometimes a small shop. Under the Swedish right of public access, allemansrätten, camping in the open is allowed almost everywhere, so many hikers carry a tent and use the huts for supplies.

## Difficulty
The northern section is moderate, with mostly gentle gradients, boardwalks over bogs and some rocky ground. Weather and isolation are the real challenges: it can snow in any month and there is no phone signal in many places.

## Practicalities
Several lakes on the southern sections are crossed by boat. Where there is no ferry, rowing boats are provided, and the rule is that at least one boat must always be left on each shore, which can mean rowing across three times. Abisko is reachable by night train from Stockholm, and buses run from Nikkaluokta to Kiruna.
