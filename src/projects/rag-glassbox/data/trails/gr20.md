# GR20

## Overview
The GR20 crosses the island of Corsica from north to south along its mountain spine. It is about 180 km long with well over 10,000 m of ascent, usually walked in 15 or 16 stages. It has a reputation as the toughest of the French Grande Randonnée trails because of its rocky terrain, scrambling and exposure.

## Route
The northern trailhead is Calenzana, near Calvi, and the southern end is Conca, near Porto-Vecchio. The village of Vizzavona sits roughly in the middle and splits the route into a northern half, which is steeper and more technical, and a southern half, which is greener and gentler. Many walkers only have time for one half and choose based on that difference. The famous Cirque de la Solitude section in the north was closed after a fatal rockfall in 2015 and replaced by a different variant.

## When to go
The usual season is June to late September. In June, snow can remain on northern passes. July and August are hot and crowded, and afternoon thunderstorms are common on the ridges, so stages typically start at dawn. September brings cooler air and fewer walkers.

## Where to stay
Mountain refuges run by the Corsican regional nature park are spaced a stage apart. Many walkers camp in the designated areas next to the refuges, because the dormitories are small and fill quickly. Wild camping on the route is not allowed. Bergeries, traditional shepherd huts, sell cheese, bread and simple meals along the way.

## Difficulty
This is the most demanding trail in this collection. Hands are needed on chains and slabs, the granite is sharp, and the path is often a boulder field rather than a track. A head for heights and a light pack make a large difference. Turning back on stormy days is common and wise.

## Practicalities
Resupply is limited, so carry two or three days of food between villages or buy from refuges and bergeries. Water sources can be scarce late in the season. Bus and train connections reach Calenzana via Calvi and Conca via Porto-Vecchio, and Vizzavona has a railway station, which makes it the easiest point to join or leave the trail.
