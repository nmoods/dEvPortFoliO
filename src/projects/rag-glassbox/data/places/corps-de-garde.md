# Corps de Garde

## Overview
Corps de Garde is a mountain of about 720 m that towers over Rose Hill, Beau Bassin and Quatre Bornes. From its ridge you see the whole central plateau, the west coast and, on clear days, Port Louis.

## Route
The classic route is a loop above Rose Hill. One detailed guide describes it as about 7 km with more than 600 m of ascent, and other sources give 2 to 4 hours. The path is marked with white stones up to the ridge.

## Difficulty
It is not really a beginner hike. The climb starts steeply, with rocky steps where you need your hands, and the slopes near the summit are challenging. Sources rate it medium to hard.

## When to go
The route has very little shade, so avoid the middle of the day. May to November is cooler; in summer, go at first light.

## Practicalities
It is free and no guide is needed. Take about two litres of water per person, sun protection and shoes with good grip.
