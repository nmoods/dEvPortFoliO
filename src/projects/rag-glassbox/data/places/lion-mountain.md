# Lion Mountain

## Overview
Lion Mountain, Montagne du Lion, rises to about 480 m above Vieux Grand Port, near Mahébourg on the south-east coast. Seen from the coast road, its ridge looks like a resting lion. It is the best-known hike in the east of the island.

## Route
The trail starts in Vieux Grand Port, near the police station, and crosses sugar cane fields before climbing the lion's spine. The return hike is about 4.5 to 5 km and usually takes 2 to 3 hours. From the top you look over the bay of Grand Port, the lagoon and the islets offshore.

## Difficulty
Sources rate it moderate to hard. The first part of the climb follows a rocky spine where you use your hands as well as your legs, with a few easy but airy scrambling moves. Waymarking is limited to paint marks and ribbons, so a guide is advisable if you do not know the route.

## When to go
Hike it in dry weather only, in any season. The rock becomes dangerous when wet. There is no water on the route and little shade, so an early morning start is best.

## Practicalities
Carry all your water. Guided hikes with local guides are offered from Mahébourg and the surrounding area.
