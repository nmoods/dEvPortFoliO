# Pieter Both

## Overview
Pieter Both is the second-highest mountain in Mauritius, at about 820 m, in the Moka Range above Port Louis. It is instantly recognisable by the round boulder, the "head", balanced on top of its narrow summit spire.

## Route
A hiking path through forest and grassland leads to the shoulder below the head. The final section, up the head itself, is a rock climb rather than a hike. Guides use fixed ropes and metal rungs bolted into the basalt, and supply helmets, harnesses and ropes after a safety briefing. Allow a full day with a guided group.

## Difficulty
This is the hardest summit in this collection. Local guides describe it as the only Mauritian ascent that genuinely requires guiding, and hikers who have done it warn that it is dangerous without a guide. A head for heights is essential, and it is not suitable for beginners.

## When to go
Climb only in dry, calm weather, from May to November if possible. Wind and wet rock make the summit block unsafe, and guides cancel when conditions are poor.

## Practicalities
Book a licensed mountain guide in advance; the price usually includes all safety equipment. If you only want the views without the climb, Le Pouce next door offers a similar panorama on a straightforward trail.
