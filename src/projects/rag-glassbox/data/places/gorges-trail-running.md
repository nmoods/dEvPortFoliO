# Black River Gorges for trail running

## Overview
Black River Gorges National Park is the main trail-running training ground in Mauritius. It has about 60 km of trails, many of them hilly with challenging, technical footing, through native forest between the Pétrin plateau and the Rivière Noire entrance.

## The training ground
The park is where you train for real mountain races. Long runs on the forest loops build endurance, and the climbs out of the gorges build strength for steep ascents and technical descents. The Royal Raid, the island's longest-running trail race, held since 2006, takes place in the Black River Gorges, with distances such as 15 km, 37 km and 70 km offered in past editions. Training on its trails is the most race-specific preparation you can do on the island.

## Who it suits
Intermediate and experienced trail runners preparing for the Royal Raid, the Dodo Trail or races abroad. Beginners should start on the shorter, well-used trails from the Pétrin visitor centre and run with someone who knows the network.

## Conditions
Footing gets very muddy and slippery in the wet season. The austral winter, from May to November, is the best period for long runs. Trails are not always well marked, and there is little phone signal in places.

## Practicalities
Entry is free. Check routes with rangers at the visitor centres, carry water and a map, and tell someone your planned route.
