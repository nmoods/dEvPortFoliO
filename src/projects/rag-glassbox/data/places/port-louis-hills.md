# Port Louis hills

## Overview
The hills around Port Louis give runners in and near the capital steep training without a long drive. The city sits in a bowl under the Moka Range, so climbing starts almost from the streets.

## The training ground
Signal Mountain, with the Marie Reine de la Paix area on its slopes, is one of the more runnable climbs close to the city centre and works well for hill repeats. The trail along the Canal Anglais, an old canal path, leads up towards the plateau and suits steady uphill runs. The Pouce valley, below Le Pouce, offers longer and more challenging trails that link the city to the mountains.

## Who it suits
Runners who work in Port Louis and want hill sessions before or after work, and hikers preparing for bigger mountains such as Le Pouce, Pieter Both or Le Morne.

## Conditions
Port Louis is one of the hottest places on the island because it is sheltered from the trade winds. Train at first light or in the evening, and carry water even on short sessions.

## Practicalities
Paths near the city are well used but vary in quality. Run with others when exploring the trails in the Pouce valley for the first time.
