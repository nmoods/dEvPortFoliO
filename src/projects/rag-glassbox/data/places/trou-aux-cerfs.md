# Trou aux Cerfs crater loop

## Overview
Trou aux Cerfs is a dormant volcanic crater in Curepipe, on the central plateau. The crater is roughly 300 to 350 m across and about 80 m deep, and a road around its rim is closed to traffic. It is one of the most popular places in Mauritius to jog or walk.

## The training ground
The rim road is paved, wide and fairly flat, and one lap is about 1 to 1.2 km. That makes it ideal for easy runs, steady tempo efforts and lap-based intervals, because every lap is the same distance and there are no cars. Walkers, families and runners share the loop, especially early in the morning and in the late afternoon. Viewpoints around the rim look into the crater and, on clear days, across to the mountains of the west and the Moka Range.

## Who it suits
Beginners building up to their first continuous run, runners doing recovery or tempo sessions, and anyone who wants a safe, measured loop without traffic. It is too flat for hill training; for that, use the mountains or La Tourelle de Tamarin.

## Conditions
Curepipe is higher, cooler and wetter than the coast, so it is a good place to train in the hot months, but expect drizzle and mist. Some viewpoints have uneven natural surfaces.

## Practicalities
Access is free. Combine laps here with longer runs on the plateau roads if you need more distance.
