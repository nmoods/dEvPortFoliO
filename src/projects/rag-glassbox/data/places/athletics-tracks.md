# Athletics tracks

## Overview
For interval sessions and speed work, nothing replaces a measured track. The main athletics venues in Mauritius are the Maryse Justin Stadium in Réduit and the Stade Germain Comarmond in Bambous.

## Maryse Justin Stadium, Réduit
The stadium is named after Maryse Justin-Pyndiah, a Mauritian long-distance runner who specialised in the marathon. It is used by the Mauritius Athletics Association and hosts national championships.

## Stade Germain Comarmond, Bambous
In the west, near Bambous, this stadium is used mostly for football and athletics, and has also hosted national youth athletics championships.

## Access
Public opening hours and fees for casual runners are not published by the athletics association, whose calendars list competitions and club training. The reliable way to train on a track is to join an athletics club or to ask the Mauritius Athletics Association or the stadium administration about access.

## Who it suits
Runners doing structured speed work, such as 400 m repeats or tempo intervals, and anyone preparing for road races who wants precise pacing. For an open-access measured loop without traffic, the Trou aux Cerfs crater rim in Curepipe is the usual alternative.
