# Flat coastal runs

## Overview
When the goal is easy mileage, recovery runs or speed on the flat, the coast is the place to train. Each side of the island has its own classic route.

## West: Flic en Flac
Flic en Flac has about 8 km of white-sand beach and a flat seafront road, which makes it one of the best places on the island for beginners and for early-morning runs. Running on sand also builds strength in the feet and calves.

## East: Belle Mare
Belle Mare has a beachfront path and roughly 1.5 km of firm sand road along the beach, with quiet farm roads inland for longer runs. One mapped beachfront loop from Belle Mare is about 10 km.

## North: Mont Choisy and Grand Baie
The coastal road leading to Mont Choisy beach is quieter than the main roads. Sections of the B13 through Pereybère, Grand Baie and Mont Choisy have a narrow sidewalk or shoulder, so run there with care and at quiet times.

## South-west: Le Morne
A flat trail runs along the water at Le Morne, a scenic option for an easy run before or after hiking the mountain.

## Conditions
The coast is hot and humid for most of the year. Run at dawn or late in the day, carry water, and wear shoes on rocky beaches where sea urchins hide.
