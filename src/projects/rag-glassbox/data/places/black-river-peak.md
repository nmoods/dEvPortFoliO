# Black River Peak

## Overview
Black River Peak, Piton de la Petite Rivière Noire, is the highest point in Mauritius at about 828 m. It stands inside Black River Gorges National Park, and despite being the highest summit it is one of the shortest hikes to a major peak on the island.

## Route
The usual trailhead is on the B103 Plaine Champagne road, close to the Black River Gorges viewpoint. Parking at the trailhead fits only two or three cars, so many hikers leave the car at the viewpoint car park a short walk away. From the viewpoint side the return hike is about 3 to 3.5 km with under 200 m of climbing, and most people reach the top in about an hour to an hour and a half.

## Difficulty
The trail is mostly well maintained and easy to follow through native forest. The final section is steeper and rockier, and ropes help on the rock in places. The trailhead is not well signposted, so check a map before you go. From the summit you look down over the west coast, Tamarin bay and Le Morne.

## When to go
May to November is drier and less muddy. Start in the morning, and turn back if the summit is in cloud and the rock is wet.

## Practicalities
Entry to the national park is free and no permit is required. Guided hikes are available if you prefer not to navigate the unsigned start. Bring a light jacket: Plaine Champagne is noticeably cooler and windier than the coast.
