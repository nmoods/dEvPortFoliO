# Tamarind Falls (Seven Cascades)

## Overview
Tamarind Falls, also called the Seven Cascades or Sept Cascades, is a chain of waterfalls in a forested canyon on the central plateau, near Henrietta in the Vacoas area. It is the island's classic waterfall hike, with natural pools for a swim along the way.

## Route
Most guided groups meet at Henrietta, often at the bus station, and walk into the canyon from there. Half-day hikes of about three hours visit some of the falls; longer full-day itineraries reach more of them. Itineraries vary, and operators quote different numbers of waterfalls, so check what is included before booking.

## Difficulty
The hike is moderate, with slippery rocks, river crossings and muddy sections. The route is not waymarked and side paths multiply through the canyon, which is why local guides recommend a guide here more firmly than on any other hike, even though one is not compulsory.

## When to go
Avoid the canyon during and after heavy rain, when river levels rise quickly and the rocks are treacherous. The drier months from May to November are safest.

## Practicalities
Wear hiking shoes with good grip that can get wet, and bring a windproof layer, swimwear and water. Swimming and jumping into pools should only be done where a guide confirms it is safe.
