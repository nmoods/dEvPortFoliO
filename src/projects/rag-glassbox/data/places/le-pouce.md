# Le Pouce

## Overview
Le Pouce, "the thumb", is the third-highest mountain in Mauritius at about 812 m. Its thumb-shaped summit rises from the Moka Range directly above Port Louis, and it is one of the most popular half-day hikes on the island because it is short, well trodden and close to the capital.

## Route
The best-known trailhead is the village of Petit Verger, near Saint Pierre, where a well-marked path climbs through sugar cane fields and patches of forest. Other approaches start from the Moka side and from the Tranquebar side of Port Louis. A common return route is about 4 to 5 km, and most walkers take 2 to 4 hours depending on the starting point and their pace.

## Difficulty
The hike is easy to moderate for most of the way. The last few hundred metres to the summit are steep and rocky, and you need good balance and sometimes your hands. The reward is a 360° view over Port Louis, the northern plains and the neighbouring peak of Pieter Both.

## When to go
The drier, cooler months from May to November are the most comfortable. Start early in the morning: the path has stretches without shade. After heavy rain the upper rock section becomes slippery.

## Practicalities
No permit or guide is needed. Carry at least a litre and a half of water, a hat and shoes with good grip. Guided sunrise and sunset hikes are also offered by local operators.
