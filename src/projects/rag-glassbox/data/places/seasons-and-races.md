# Training seasons and trail races

## Seasons
The austral winter, from May to November, is the best period for hiking and running in Mauritius: it is cooler and drier, and trails are less muddy. The summer months, roughly November to April, are hot and humid with heavy rain, and cyclones can affect the island. During summer, train at dawn, shorten long efforts and stay off mountain trails after heavy rain, when rock and mud become dangerous. Always check weather warnings before heading into the mountains.

## Royal Raid (May)
The Royal Raid has been held since 2006 and is the island's longest-running trail race. It takes place in the Black River Gorges, with distances such as 15 km, 37 km and 70 km offered in past editions. It also opens the Mauritius Odyssey, a multi-stage event.

## Dodo Trail (July)
The Dodo Trail, held in July, is the island's signature trail race. The 2026 edition gathered more than 3,000 runners. Its flagship Xtreme course covers about 50 km with around 3,500 m of ascent, starting at dawn in the Le Morne area and finishing in Tamarin. Shorter formats include the Trooper 25 km, the Venture 10 km and the Rookie 5 km.

## Building a training block
A typical approach on the island combines the training grounds in this guide: easy laps at Trou aux Cerfs or on the coast, hill strength on La Tourelle de Tamarin or the Port Louis hills, and race-specific long runs in the Black River Gorges. Race dates and formats change each year, so confirm them with the organisers before planning.
