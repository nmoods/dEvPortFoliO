# Le Morne Brabant

## Overview
Le Morne Brabant is the 556 m basalt mountain on the south-western peninsula of Mauritius. It is a UNESCO World Heritage cultural landscape, inscribed in 2008, because its cliffs sheltered escaped enslaved people, the maroons, in the 18th and 19th centuries. Hike it with that history in mind.

## Route
The trail starts at a gate near the base of the mountain, where hikers sign in and out of a register for safety. The lower trail climbs to a viewpoint with a large cross, with views over the lagoon and the south-west coast. Above the cross, the final section to the summit is a steep scramble where you climb on all fours. A full return to the summit takes about 3 to 5 hours.

## Rules and access
Published opening hours differ between sources, but the gate opens early in the morning and late starts are not allowed, with the last departure in the early afternoon. Access is closed on rainy days. Sources also disagree on whether a licensed guide is required above the cross. Check the current rules on site or with a local operator before you plan the summit.

## Difficulty
To the cross, the hike is moderate but hot. The summit section is hard and exposed, and should be skipped after rain because the rock becomes slippery.

## When to go
Start at dawn. There is very little shade, and the south-west coast gets hot by mid-morning. The cooler months from May to November are the most pleasant.
