# Bras d'Eau National Park

## Overview
Bras d'Eau National Park is a coastal forest park in the north-east of Mauritius. Its trails run through forest that includes mangroves and endemic trees, and they are much flatter than the mountain trails of the west and centre.

## The training ground
Bras d'Eau is a good place for fairly easy trail running. Trails start from the visitor centre, and the gentle terrain makes it a natural first step from road running to trail running, or a place for relaxed long runs on soft ground.

## Who it suits
Beginners trying trail running for the first time, runners in the east and north of the island, and anyone who wants shaded, quiet running away from traffic without technical terrain.

## Conditions
Being on the coast, it is warm and humid; the forest gives more shade than beach routes. After heavy rain, parts of the trails can be muddy.

## Practicalities
Pick up information at the visitor centre before heading out, and stay on the marked trails to protect the forest.
