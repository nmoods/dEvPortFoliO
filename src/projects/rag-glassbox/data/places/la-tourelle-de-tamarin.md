# La Tourelle de Tamarin

## Overview
La Tourelle de Tamarin is a steep mountain less than a kilometre from the coast at Tamarin, on the west of the island. Runners use it as a brutal hill-training ground: about 560 m of ascent over roughly 4.4 km return.

## The training ground
This is a place for vertical training, not for setting times. Repeats on the steepest part build the climbing strength needed for mountain races. Fixed ropes equip some of the steps, so expect to use your hands. The descent is as important to practise as the climb: take it carefully, especially when tired.

## Who it suits
Trail runners and hikers preparing for races with a lot of climbing, such as the Dodo Trail or the Royal Raid. It is not a beginner route; build up on easier hills first, such as Le Pouce or the Port Louis hills.

## Conditions
The west coast is hot and dry, and the slope offers little shade. Train at dawn or late in the afternoon. Darkness falls quickly in Mauritius, in about twenty minutes, between about 5:45 pm and 7 pm depending on the season, so carry a headlamp on evening sessions.

## Practicalities
Bring plenty of water and gloves for the rope sections. Avoid it after rain, when the steps become slippery.
